How keys are handled

API keys are stored encrypted in your WordPress database and are only ever used server-side when calling the provider's API. They are never printed into HTML, never sent to the browser, and never written to logs.

How switching works

Configure as many providers as you like in AI Settings, test each connection, then pick the active one per task. Your audits, history and settings stay untouched when you switch.

A note on Meta: Meta's AI endpoints and model availability change over time and vary by region and account. Verify the endpoint and model names against your provider's documentation — LocalBoost lets you configure the base URL and model explicitly for exactly this reason.
A note on self-hosted models: the self-hosted provider intentionally allows localhost / 127.0.0.1 targets (that's the point of Ollama). Only point it at endpoints you control.
