Built for local businesses

LocalBoost AI is designed for the businesses that live or die by local search — the dentist on Main Street, not the multinational. If your customers search for what you do near them, this is for you.

Dentists & clinics

Make each treatment page findable: clear titles, local FAQ schema, and accurate business hours in structured data.

Plumbers & electricians

Service-area pages that explain coverage honestly, with click-to-call calls to action you approve first.

Restaurants & cafés

Menus, opening hours and location details kept consistent, with schema search engines understand.

Retail shops

Category and product pages with unique meta descriptions instead of duplicates.

Salons & barbershops

Service descriptions and booking calls to action, reviewed by you before anything goes live.

Law firms & accountants

Practice-area pages with careful, human-reviewed wording — the AI drafts, you keep the professional tone.

Real estate agencies

Neighborhood and listing pages with consistent local signals and valid schema.

Hotels & guesthouses

Room and amenity pages with multilingual-ready structure and accurate local info.

Auto repair shops

Service pages that answer the questions local drivers actually search for.

Any local business

If customers find you by searching for what you do + where you are, LocalBoost fits your WordPress site.

Honest note: LocalBoost improves your pages; it can't guarantee rankings. What it can do is make sure your site isn't losing local customers over fixable, basic issues.
