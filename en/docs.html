Documentation

Get LocalBoost AI installed, connected, and auditing in minutes.

Requirements

  • WordPress 6.0 or newer
  • PHP 7.4 or newer
  • An API key from at least one AI provider — or a self-hosted Ollama-compatible model (no key needed)

Installation

  1. Download the plugin ZIP (v1.0.0).
  2. In WordPress, go to Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin, choose the ZIP, and click Install Now, then Activate.
  3. The 7-step onboarding starts automatically: business profile, AI provider connection, and your first audit.

Connecting an AI provider

  1. Go to LocalBoost AI → AI Settings.
  2. Choose a provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Meta, or Self-hosted).
  3. Paste your API key (self-hosted needs no key — just the endpoint URL) and click Test connection.
  4. Set it as active. You can configure several and switch anytime.

Keys are encrypted at rest and never exposed to the browser.

Running your first audit

  1. Go to LocalBoost AI → SEO Audit, pick a page or post, and run the audit.
  2. Read your 0–100 LocalBoost SEO Score and the issue list.
  3. For any fixable issue, click Fix with AI, review the side-by-side preview, edit if you like, then Apply.

Business profile & schema

Under LocalBoost AI → Local SEO, enter your name, address, phone, hours, services and service area once. LocalBoost outputs valid JSON-LD on your site so search engines understand your business.

Monitoring & reports

Under Settings, enable scheduled audits (weekly or monthly). You'll get email notifications when audits complete or critical issues appear, and you can export CSV reports from the Reports page. (PDF export is not available.)

Usage limits

Free tier, per month: 25 audits, 10 AI generations, 5 reports, 10 content generations. Your remaining quota is always visible on the dashboard.

What LocalBoost doesn't do

  • It never changes or publishes content automatically — you approve everything.
  • It doesn't show keyword search volumes (we won't invent data).
  • It doesn't create doorway pages or send telemetry.
  • It doesn't replace an SEO professional's judgment — it drafts, you decide.

Troubleshooting

  • Provider test fails: double-check the key, the selected model name, and (for Meta/self-hosted) the base URL.
  • Quota reached: the dashboard shows remaining quota; limits reset monthly.
  • Interface language: the admin UI is English today; a translation template (.pot) ships for translators, with French, German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch and Portuguese planned.
