Frequently asked questions

Straight answers — including the things we don't do.

Will LocalBoost AI get me to #1 on Google?+
No — and be skeptical of any tool that promises that. Rankings depend on competition, reviews, links, and many factors no plugin controls. LocalBoost fixes the on-page and local issues that are actually in your hands.
Does it change my content automatically?+
Never. Every AI suggestion appears as a preview next to the current version. Nothing is applied, saved, or published until you click apply — and important content is never overwritten silently.
Does it show keyword search volume?+
No. We don't have licensed search-volume data, and we refuse to invent it. The keyword assistant suggests ideas based on your business and location, clearly labeled as ideas — not metrics.
Which AI providers can I use?+
OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Meta, or any self-hosted Ollama-compatible endpoint. You bring your own API key (except self-hosted, which is keyless) and can switch providers anytime.
Is my API key safe?+
Keys are encrypted at rest, used server-side only, and never exposed to frontend JavaScript, HTML, or logs.
Does it work with Yoast SEO or Rank Math?+
Yes, alongside them. LocalBoost reads your existing meta values from Yoast/Rank Math when auditing, and stores its own suggestions separately — it won't silently overwrite what those plugins manage.
What does the free tier include?+
Per month: 25 audits, 10 AI generations, 5 reports, and 10 content generations — plus all providers, JSON-LD schema, monitoring and email alerts. No payment details required.
Is there really no checkout?+
Correct. There is no payment flow in the plugin or on this site. Pro is in development; its pricing will be announced before anything is ever charged.
Can I export reports as PDF?+
Not currently — reports export as CSV, which opens in any spreadsheet. PDF export is not on the short-term roadmap.
What languages does the plugin support?+
The admin interface is English today. A translation template (.pot) ships with the plugin, and French, German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch and Portuguese are planned.
Does it work on multisite or for agencies managing many sites?+
That's on the roadmap, not in v1.0.0. Today LocalBoost AI is a single-site plugin.
What are the technical requirements?+
WordPress 6.0+ and PHP 7.4+. No other dependencies — it's a standard WordPress plugin ZIP.
