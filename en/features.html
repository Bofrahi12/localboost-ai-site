Features

One plugin for the whole local SEO loop: audit, fix, publish-ready schema, content help, and monitoring — all inside WordPress.

Local SEO audit & 0–100 score

Run an audit on any page or post and get the LocalBoost SEO Score — a single understandable number — plus a list of concrete issues grouped by severity: missing meta description, thin content, weak headings, missing local signals, and more. Each issue explains what it is and why it matters.

Fix with AI — preview before apply

Every fixable issue has a “Fix with AI” action. The AI drafts a new title, meta description, FAQ block or call to action, and you see the current version next to the suggestion. Apply only what you approve. Important content is never overwritten automatically.

Local business profile & valid JSON-LD

Enter your business details once — name, address, phone, hours, services, service area — and LocalBoost generates valid JSON-LD schema for your site, so search engines can understand your local business.

Content & keyword assistant

Draft service descriptions, introductions and FAQ content, and brainstorm keyword ideas around your business and city. Honest by design: we show ideas, never fabricated search-volume numbers.

Monitoring, reports & email alerts

Schedule automatic re-audits, watch your score history on the dashboard, export CSV reports, and get email notifications when an audit finishes or a critical issue appears.

Five AI providers, your key

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Meta, or a self-hosted Ollama-compatible model. You supply the API key; it is encrypted at rest and never exposed to frontend JavaScript. Switch providers anytime.

Seven-step onboarding

A guided setup walks you through the business profile, provider connection, and your first audit — so the plugin is useful within minutes, not hours.

Fair usage limits

The free tier includes monthly quotas for audits, AI generations, reports and content tools. Usage is tracked transparently in the dashboard, and you'll always see what's left.

What LocalBoost doesn't do: it never auto-publishes content, never invents keyword search volumes, never creates doorway pages, and never sends telemetry anywhere. Your site stays yours.
