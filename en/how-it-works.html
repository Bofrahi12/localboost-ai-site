How it works

Six calm steps. The AI drafts, you decide. Nothing on your site changes without your explicit approval.

1. Scan

Pick any page or post and run a local SEO audit. LocalBoost checks titles, meta descriptions, headings, content depth, images, local business signals and technical basics, then rolls everything into a single 0–100 LocalBoost SEO Score. The free tier includes 25 audits per month.

2. Explain

Each issue is presented in plain language: what was found, why it matters for local search, and what a good fix looks like. No SEO jargon required — a dentist or plumber should understand every line.

3. Generate

For issues that AI can fix, press “Fix with AI”. LocalBoost sends the page context to the AI provider you configured — OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Meta, or your own self-hosted model — and drafts the new title, meta description, FAQ, or call to action. The free tier includes 10 AI generations per month.

4. Review

This is the step other tools skip. You see the current text side by side with the AI suggestion, with differences clear. Edit the suggestion yourself if you like. Nothing is saved, published, or applied at this stage.

5. Apply

Happy with the suggestion? One click applies it. Changes are sanitized, recorded in a change history (what changed, when, and with which provider), and your previous content stays recoverable through WordPress revisions. If Yoast SEO or Rank Math manages a field, LocalBoost reads their values and stores its own suggestions separately — it won't silently fight your SEO plugin.

6. Monitor

Schedule automatic re-audits (weekly or monthly) to watch scores move over time. Get an email when an audit completes or a critical issue appears, and export CSV reports to share with a team or client.

Requirements: WordPress 6.0 or newer, PHP 7.4 or newer, and an API key from at least one AI provider (or a self-hosted model — no key needed).
