The workflow

From scan to steady improvement in six steps

A calm, human-in-the-loop process: the AI does the drafting, you make the decisions.

  1. Scan

    Run a local SEO audit on any page and get a clear 0–100 LocalBoost SEO Score plus a list of concrete issues.

    The plugin checks your content — never guesses.

  2. Explain

    Every issue comes with a plain-language explanation of what it is and why it matters for local search.

    No jargon, no black box.

  3. Generate

    AI drafts the fix — titles, meta descriptions, FAQs, calls to action — using the provider you choose.

    OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Meta, or self-hosted.

  4. Review

    Compare the current version side by side with the AI suggestion. Nothing is applied without your approval.

    You stay in control.

  5. Apply

    Apply with one click. Changes are previewed, sanitized, and recorded so you can review what changed.

    Safe by design.

  6. Monitor

    Scheduled re-audits track your score over time and email you when a page needs attention.

    Set it, then check in.

FAQ

Quick answers

Will LocalBoost guarantee me #1 on Google?+
No — and you should distrust any tool that promises that. LocalBoost helps you fix real on-page and local SEO issues; rankings depend on many factors outside any plugin's control.
Does it publish or change my content automatically?+
Never. Every AI suggestion is shown as a preview next to the current version, and nothing changes until you click apply.
Does it show keyword search volumes?+
No. We don't have access to real search-volume data and we won't invent it. The keyword tool suggests ideas based on your business and location — clearly labeled as ideas, not metrics.
