Will the free tier stay free?+
Yes. The free tier with the limits above is part of the product. Pro will add higher limits and extras for those who need them.

LocalBoost AI doesn't sell AI usage. You connect your own provider account (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Meta) and pay that provider directly under their terms — or run a self-hosted model for free. LocalBoost only counts plugin-side usage against the tiers above.
