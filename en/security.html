Security & privacy

How LocalBoost AI protects your keys, your content, and your visitors.

API keys, locked down

Provider API keys are encrypted at rest in your WordPress database. They are used server-side only, and are never printed into HTML, never sent to frontend JavaScript, and never written to logs.

Access control

All admin screens and API endpoints require administrator capabilities. Content-changing actions verify you can edit the specific post — not just that you're logged in.

Input & output safety

Every user input is validated, sanitized, and capability-checked; every output is escaped. AI-generated text is sanitized before it's shown or applied, and CSV exports neutralize spreadsheet formula injection.

Custom AI endpoints

If you point the self-hosted provider at a custom URL, LocalBoost validates the scheme and host. localhost is allowed by design for Ollama — only use endpoints you control.

No telemetry

LocalBoost AI sends no usage data, no analytics, and no content anywhere except to the AI provider you explicitly configured, for the requests you explicitly make.

Your data stays yours

Audits, history, business details and reports live in your own WordPress database. Deactivating or uninstalling follows WordPress conventions — your content is never held hostage.

Responsible disclosure: found a security issue? During the beta, report it through the beta channel after joining below. Please don't post exploit details publicly before we've had a chance to fix them.
